Shows the storage filters of every stockpile, shelf and storage group on the map side by side in one table, so you can see and change where everything goes without opening each storage one by one.

[h2]Opening it[/h2]
Click the [b]Storage[/b] button on the main button bar. In the mod settings it can be moved to an [b]Overview[/b] tab instead, the leftmost tab of a selected stockpile or storage building.

[h2]The table[/h2][list]
[*]The item tree on the left is the vanilla storage tree. Each storage is a column of the usual checkboxes, and opening a category opens it for every column at once. Items a storage can never hold leave its cell empty.
[*]Ticking a box works exactly like the vanilla storage tab, including click-and-drag painting.
[*]Priority, hit points and quality are rows at the top: click the priority bars for the vanilla priority menu, and drag the ends of the vertical bars to set the ranges.
[*]Storages with identical settings are merged into one column (can be turned off). Changes in a merged column apply to all of them. Another option merges by item filters only, to change the items of similar storages together even when their priority, hit points or quality differ.
[*]Click a storage name to show it on the map to the right of the table. Names of the storages in that map view are brightened. Right-click it to rename it, or to copy or paste its settings with the vanilla clipboard.
[/list]

[h2]Finding what to change[/h2][list]
[*][b]Show:[/b] all items, only items that are on the map, or only items no storage accepts, for example new items from a mod you just added.
[*][b]Search:[/b] filter the items by name, and the storages by name.
[*][b]Marks:[/b] tick the box at the left of items or categories, and the storages that accept them move to the left: highest priority first, then the ones accepting the most. The rest are dimmed behind a divider but can still be ticked.
[*][b]Blue outline:[/b] the storage haulers actually take an item to, which is the highest priority storage that accepts it. On a tie all are outlined; the nearest one is used.
[*][b]Light blue cell:[/b] the storage currently holds that item. Hover it for the count.
[*][b]Dimmed row:[/b] no storage accepts that item.
[*]The number next to an item is how many are on the map.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Tested on RimWorld 1.7.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[/list]
